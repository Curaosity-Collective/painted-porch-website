# Landing Page Copy Audit — Envision ShIFt

**Page:** onthepaintedporch.com/envision-shift
**Reviewed:** 2026-11-13
**Against:** no-ai-slop, stop-slop, humanizer, copywriting, viral-hooks

**Note on scope:** `docs/archive/landing-page-copy-2026-01.md` in this repo is the January 2026 draft, marked superseded on 2026-11-13. It quotes a $697 diagnostic and an $18,000–$69,000 Design Session, neither of which is a current offer. This audit covers the live page, which is the copy that converts today. The repo file should be deleted or archived, not edited.

---

## 1. Conversion issues (fix first)

### 1.1 The hero has no call to action

The page opens with the H1, a subhead, a video, and an image. The first button a visitor can click is "See what clarity could save," several screens down, and the actual booking CTA sits at the very bottom.

**Fix:** put "Start with a Clarity Call" in the hero, directly under the subhead. Every additional scroll before the first CTA costs conversions on a $24,000 offer where the first step is a $750 call.

### 1.2 The hero subhead describes the category, not the reader's situation

**Current:** "Every epic shIFt begins at a threshold: an honest appraisal of what your organization can realistically carry. An interview-led organizational 360 determines which change and direction make sense to pursue next."

Two sentences, both about the offer. Neither names the moment the visitor is in.

**Fix:** "Before your next initiative, find out what your organization can actually carry. Six interviews, executive to frontline, show which change makes sense to pursue next."

That leads with the reader's decision, then says what the product is in concrete terms (six interviews, executive to frontline) rather than in category language (interview-led organizational 360).

### 1.3 The closing header is generic

**Current:** "Ready to Begin?"
**Fix:** "Start with Clarity." It names what the visitor gets and matches the page's own language.

## 3. What already works

- **The 88% PwC stat** is cited, specific, and positioned well. It earns the paragraph that follows.
- **"Clarity is valuable even when it changes the plan"** is the strongest line on the page. It answers the buyer's real fear — that an appraisal might tell them to stop — before they can voice it.
- **"See the Cost in Your Own Numbers"** is a strong mid-page hook because it's interactive and specific.
- **The four P.A.T.H. Compass outputs** are concrete and distinct. No triad inflation; each item says something different.
- **The meta description** is specific and carries the price. Most pages hide it.

## 4. Suggested order of work

1. Add the hero CTA (1.1). Highest impact, smallest change.
2. Rewrite the hero subhead (1.2). Second highest impact.
3. Fix 2.1 and 2.2. Two-line edits, immediate improvement.
4. Strip the decorative periods (2.3, 2.4).
5. Vary "carry" (2.5) and thin the dashes (2.6).
6. Rename the closing header (1.3).
